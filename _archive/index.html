The long table, ground floor

Est. in a corner house

A picnic cloth
thrown over
a palazzo.

Oro means gold. We built the house around that one idea: warm cove light that never goes blue, brass on the door handles, and a red gingham skin pulled tight over plaster cornices. Downstairs is the espresso bar and the long table. Upstairs, past the crimson staircase, is quieter — for long lunches and slow afternoons.
